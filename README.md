# EV Trailer

A map of public DC fast chargers (50 kW and above) in Canada and the United States, for people towing a trailer.

Open the site at https://duss48.github.io/ev-trailer/. The page loads the station list from `stations-na.json` in this folder.

## Data sources

- **Open Charge Map** is the source of the station list: public DC chargers of 50 kW or more in Canada and the United States.
- **supercharge.info** is the source of the trailer-friendly Supercharger list.
- **Circuit électrique** trailer sites come from the trailer list in the owner's app screenshots.

## Colour tags and notes

Colour tags and notes are stored only on your device, in this browser. They are not uploaded to this site. Use Export and Import on the page if you want a copy to keep or move to another device.

A charger on the trailer-friendly list starts out green. Yellow is only a tag you set yourself. The first time this version loads, an older yellow tag on a listed trailer-friendly charger is cleared so that charger shows green again. Notes are left as they are.
